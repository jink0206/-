## 영어 자막(참고)
A: You clocked out. But is your mind still at the office?
B: I thought work ended when I left.
Studs Terkel wrote that work is "a search for daily meaning as well as daily bread, for recognition as well as cash."
A night when the words you didn't say in the meeting keep circling.
Maybe it means you worked that sincerely.
Stamp a clock-out on that sincerity, too.
